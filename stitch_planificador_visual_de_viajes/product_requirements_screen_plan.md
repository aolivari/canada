# Travel Planning App: "VacaVibes" (Provisional Name)

## Concept
A cheerful, visual travel itinerary planner that combines the power of a dashboard with a warm, "coqueto" aesthetic. It caters to all types of travelers (solo, families, couples) by providing a modular and inspiring organization tool.

## Key Features
1. **Visual Timeline & Calendar:** A central hub to see daily activities, flight times, and hotel check-ins.
2. **Price Breakdown:** A clear, visual budget tracker to manage costs for accommodation, activities, and food.
3. **Weather Integration:** Real-time forecasts for the destination to help plan daily outfits and outdoor activities.
4. **Accommodation & Activity Cards:** Rich visual cards with photos, addresses, and booking details.
5. **Packing List:** A "coqueto" checklist to ensure nothing is forgotten.

## Screen List (Proposal)
1. **Dashboard / My Trips:** A high-level view of upcoming adventures with beautiful destination covers.
2. **Itinerary Planner (The "Core"):** A split view with a vertical timeline on one side and a detailed map or weather/budget widgets on the other.
3. **Budget & Expenses:** A dedicated page with charts and a breakdown of costs per category.
4. **Trip Discovery / New Trip:** A joyful onboarding flow to set up a new destination, dates, and traveler type.
